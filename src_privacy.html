Privacy Policy

Hexbound Company, by RoninSoft · Effective 2 November 2026

The short version: Hexbound Company collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no internet permission. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What the game stores

The game keeps your progress (your company, town, unlocks and settings) and a small diagnostics log in its private storage on your device. None of it is sent anywhere by the game. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The in-game "Reset save data" option deletes your progress while the game stays installed.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or use network access. On Android the app declares no internet permission, so it cannot connect to anything.
  • It does not collect names, email addresses, identifiers, location, contacts, or anything else about you or your device.
  • It does not show ads, contain in-app purchases, or include third-party analytics or tracking.

Copy diagnostics

Settings has a "Copy diagnostics" button. It puts a short text on your clipboard: the build number, your device model and graphics driver, and the last few lines of the game's own log. Nothing happens with that text unless you paste and send it yourself, for example in a bug report. It never includes personal information.

When you contact us

If you email support@roninsoft.io or post on our Discord server, we see what you send and use it to reply and to improve the game. Email is kept as long as it is useful for support. The Discord server is operated under Discord's privacy policy.

App stores

When you install the game from Google Play, Google processes the install and any crash or usage statistics it gathers on its own under Google's privacy policy. We only see the aggregated figures Google shows developers, never anything that identifies you.

Children

Hexbound Company is a cartoon strategy game suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version of the game ever starts handling data differently, for example with online features, this policy will be updated first and the change noted here with a new effective date.
